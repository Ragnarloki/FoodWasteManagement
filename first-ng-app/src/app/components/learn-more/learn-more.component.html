Learn More About Food Waste Reduction

Food waste is a global issue affecting millions of lives. Below are some key ways to help reduce food waste and contribute to a sustainable future.

📌 Why Reduce Food Waste?

By reducing food waste, we can save money, reduce environmental impact, and help feed those in need.

🔹 Practical Steps to Reduce Food Waste

  • ✅ Buy What You Need

    Avoid impulse buying and only purchase necessary groceries.

  • ✅ Proper Storage

    Store food in the right conditions to extend its shelf life.

  • ✅ Creative Cooking

    Use leftovers creatively to make new meals instead of throwing them away.

  • ✅ Support Food Donation Programs

    Donate extra food to local shelters and organizations.
