Food Waste Awareness

Every year, tons of food are wasted while millions go hungry. You can help reduce waste by:

  • Planning Meals

    Organize your meals for the week to avoid over-purchasing and ensure all food is utilized.

  • Storing Food Properly

    Use airtight containers and appropriate refrigeration to extend the shelf life of your food.

  • Donating Excess Food

    Share surplus non-perishable items with local food banks to support those in need.

  • Composting Food Scraps

    Transform organic waste into nutrient-rich compost for gardening, reducing landfill contributions.
